## Verification standard used in this pack

- **Verbatim:** judging weights, deadlines, prizes, rules and form fields were extracted by script from the primary CMS / form JSON. They are quoted, never retyped.
- **Contract addresses:** every address marked verified was checked on BSC mainnet with read-only RPC calls: `symbol()`/`decimals()` for tokens and `eth_getCode` for contracts. Tables were generated by script from those results.
  - Cross-checks: Ondo's own registry vs Binance's list (457/459 match), xStocks API vs Binance's list (128/128 match), eligible bStock list vs Binance live list (68/68 match).
- **Sub-agent captures** are leads until checked. Items the lead did not re-read are tagged *(sub-agent)* inline.
- **Raw snapshots are not committed** (third-party copyright). Re-fetch URLs and SHA-256 prefixes are below. A future session can reproduce every fact.
